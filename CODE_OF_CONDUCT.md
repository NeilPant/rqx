# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The whole of it comes down to: argue about the code, not
about the person writing it.

## What that means in practice

**Assume the other person is trying.** A bad rule, a wrong benchmark, a pull request
that misses the point — these are ordinary. Say what is wrong with the work and what
would make it right. "This is quadratic on a large file, here is the input that shows
it" is a review. "Did you even test this" is not.

**Be direct.** A vague, polite non-answer wastes more of someone's time than a blunt
one. Directness is not licence to be unpleasant, and unpleasantness is not a
substitute for being right.

**Not everyone is a native English speaker, a Rust expert, or on your timezone.**
Read past the grammar to the point. Answer the question that was meant.

**Say when you are guessing.** rqx makes claims about other people's code for a
living. A maintainer who overstates a benchmark, a precision number, or what a rule
detects is doing the same damage the tool exists to prevent.

## What will get you removed

Harassment, in the issue tracker or anywhere it follows someone from here: personal
attacks, slurs, sexual attention nobody asked for, sustained derailing, publishing
someone's private information, or persisting with any of it after being asked to
stop. Discrimination on any of the usual grounds, and any of the unusual ones.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at <jadhamwi4@gmail.com>. Say what happened and where; a link is
enough. Reports stay between you and the maintainer, including from the person you
are reporting, unless you say otherwise.

You will get an answer. Depending on what happened, the outcome is a private word, an
edited or deleted comment, or a block. If the person you need to report is the
maintainer, say so publicly in an issue — a project this size has no committee to
appeal to, and a public record is the only check that exists.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
and any space where you are recognisably here on its behalf.
