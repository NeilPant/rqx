# Code of Conduct

Discussion in this project is about the code, not about the person who wrote it.

## What that means in practice

Assume good faith. A flawed rule, an incorrect benchmark, or a pull request that
misses its goal is an ordinary event. Review comments state what is wrong with the
work and what would fix it. For example, "This is quadratic on a large file, here is
the input that shows it" is a review comment. "Did you even test this" is not.

Be direct. A vague answer costs the reader more time than a blunt one. Directness
does not extend to unpleasantness.

Contributors differ in first language, Rust experience and timezone. Read for the
point of a message and answer the question that was meant.

State uncertainty. rqx makes claims about other people's code, so a maintainer who
overstates a benchmark, a precision number, or what a rule detects is making the
kind of error the tool exists to find.

## What will get you removed

Harassment, in the issue tracker or in any place where it follows a person from this
project, results in removal. Harassment includes personal attacks, slurs, unwanted
sexual attention, sustained derailing of discussions, publishing someone's private
information, and continuing any of these after being asked to stop. Discrimination on
any ground, including grounds not commonly listed, also results in removal.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at <jadhamwi4@gmail.com>. Describe what happened and where. A
link is enough. Reports stay between the reporter and the maintainer, including from
the person being reported, unless the reporter says otherwise.

Every report gets a response. Depending on what happened, the outcome is a private
conversation, an edited or deleted comment, or a block. If the report concerns the
maintainer, post it publicly in an issue. A project of this size has no committee to
appeal to, and a public record is the only external check.

## Scope

This applies to every place where the project happens: issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit messages, and any space where a person is recognisably acting
on behalf of the project.
